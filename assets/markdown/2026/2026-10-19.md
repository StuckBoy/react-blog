## Always Be Learning
A lesson I have experienced time and time again is that no one knows everything.
More importantly, the first step to learning something is knowing that you don't
know something. As such, this new blog has been born out of the desire to learn
how to utilize Markdown within a React/Typescript project. I realized that I 
lack a lot of experience with modern web development. So, what better way to 
shore up that lack of knowledge than with the ramblings of a man on the 
precipice (or perhaps within the midst) of his midlife crisis.
